Odd jobs, done by students

Need a hand? Post a job. Have free time? Take one. Jobs are posted by students and by our team.

  • Grocery run

    Someone picks up your list from the grocery lounge and brings it to you.

  • Waiting in line

    Hold your spot in a queue while you finish class or work.

  • Printing

    Send your files and get them printed and delivered.

  • Something else

    Post any small task another student can handle.

How it works

  1. Post a job

    Describe the task, where and when you need it, and the pay.

  2. A student takes it

    Another student signs up for the job you posted.

  3. Get it done

    Meet in a public spot on campus and finish the task.

Made by students, for students

We started this as a school project to solve two everyday student problems: finding a healthy meal between classes, and finding time for small errands. Our meals are ready to eat, and our job board connects students who need help with students who have time to give it.
